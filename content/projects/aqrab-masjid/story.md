Share your location, or search a city or area, and get the closest mosques and prayer spaces with directions. Your location is used only to find nearby places; nothing is stored.

**How it was built.** A small web app on top of a places API with a privacy-first flow: the location request explains itself before the browser prompt appears, and search works without location for people who prefer not to share it.

**What I learned.** For a utility people open in a hurry, the first screen has to do one thing. Everything else moved behind search.
